# Game strategies against the model

Generated by `python -m momofraud.serving.strategies`. Each scripted strategy is played
6 times on the demo network, through the same game engine as the
web page.
Profit is what reached the player's accounts minus the cost of numbers (500 FCFA new, 10,000 rented).

| Strategy | Mean profit (FCFA) | Caught | Never flagged | Scored attempts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| takeover: rush | -500 | 100% | 0% | 1.0 |
| takeover: small amounts, straight away | -500 | 100% | 0% | 1.2 |
| takeover: wait 3 days, new number | -500 | 100% | 0% | 1.5 |
| takeover: wait 3 days, rented account | 83,667 | 0% | 100% | 20.0 |
| rogue agent: 100 requests in 10 min | 4,167 | 100% | 0% | 4.3 |
| rogue agent: 10 local requests a day | 500 | 0% | 50% | 0.8 |
| sent by mistake: fresh numbers | -1,500 | 100% | 0% | 3.0 |
| sent by mistake: rented numbers | 59,500 | 0% | 100% | 14.0 |

"Never flagged" means not a single review or block in the whole game: the model did not see
the scam at all. Those strategies are its blind spots.
