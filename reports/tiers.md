# The four difficulty levels

Generated by `python -m momofraud.tiers`. Each level is a separately trained LightGBM model.
Thresholds: 90% precision to block, 50% to review, chosen on each model's own validation days.
Recall is measured at the review threshold on the test days (75-89).

| Level | Knows | PR-AUC (adaptive) | Recall (adaptive) | False alarms / 10k | Rented attacks caught |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rookie | The transaction alone: amount, balance, time of day. | 0.034 | 0% | 4.4 | 0% |
| Analyst | Also the sender's habits and whether they have paid this person before. | 0.374 | 45% | 12.1 | 11% |
| Expert | All 33 behavioural features: phones, SIM swaps, receiving accounts, agents. | 0.861 | 85% | 15.1 | 27% |
| Elite | The same features, retrained on rented-account attacks after the game exposed them. | 0.847 | 84% | 20.7 | 82% |

Recall by scenario at the review threshold, adaptive world:

| Level | account_takeover | withdrawal_request | wrong_transfer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rookie | 0% | 2% | 0% |
| Analyst | 66% | 2% | 9% |
| Expert | 100% | 90% | 37% |
| Elite | 100% | 90% | 35% |
